Two accounts, one ledger

Built around who actually touches the shop

Instead of one generic login, the platform gives each person exactly the view their role needs — Customer or Shop Owner.

01 — Customer

Check stock & Scan to pay

Search a product and see live availability at nearby shops, then click to scan the shop's UPI QR code to pay instantly.

  • Search by product name or shop
  • Live in-stock / low / out status from MongoDB
  • Instant UPI QR payment scanner
02 — Business Owner

Upload Scanner & Manage Counter

Upload your own GPay / PhonePe / Paytm scanner image, change stock status, and monitor incoming customer payments.

  • Upload your custom UPI QR code scanner
  • Add, edit stock, and delete store items
  • Live customer transaction ledger
03 — Node & Mongo Backend

Production REST API

Powered by Express.js, JWT security, and MongoDB Mongoose database schemas.

  • JWT-protected route middlewares
  • Role-based access (Owner vs Customer)
  • Shareable on local Wi-Fi or Internet
